Who operates Pocket

This Pocket instance is operated by Simone Saruggia for his personal budgeting. Contact: simosaruggia@gmail.com.

Data stored

Pocket stores expenses, dates, descriptions, categories, trip tags and budgets. If you connect a bank, it also stores bank account identifiers, consent/session information, transaction references, amounts, currencies and descriptions needed for importing and duplicate detection. Bank connection records and import drafts are encrypted in the application database; saved expenses remain in the existing budget database.

Bank access

Enable Banking provides read-only account-information access after you authorize it through its consent flow and your bank. Pocket does not collect your bank login password or card security code, and this integration cannot initiate payments. Access lasts until consent expires or you disconnect. When you press Refresh now, Pocket forwards your IP address and browser request information to Enable Banking to identify the refresh as user-requested. These details are not stored in the Pocket database.

Service providers

Railway hosts this app and its persistent database. Enable Banking and Revolut process the bank connection under their own policies. If you enable GPT category suggestions, transaction descriptions and category codes are sent to OpenAI, with the API storage option disabled. Pocket does not send bank credentials, account numbers or balances for categorisation. Provider security/abuse-monitoring retention may still apply. Free-text descriptions can contain personal information.

For currency estimates, Pocket requests currency pairs and dates from Frankfurter and ArgentinaDatos. It does not send your merchant descriptions or account details to those rate services. Amounts supplied by the bank are used as the booked charge; unsupported currencies remain for review.

Storage and your choices

Imported expenses are kept until you delete them. Dismissed import references are retained to prevent repeat imports. Disconnect stops future access; it does not delete your saved budget. Contact the operator to request an export or deletion of connection/import data. Operational backups may retain data until replaced or removed. Railway and other providers may process data outside your country according to their service terms.

Access and cookies

Pocket uses an essential private-access session cookie and a short-lived bank-connection cookie. Anyone with your private Pocket link can access this instance; keep the link private. No advertising cookies are added by Pocket.
