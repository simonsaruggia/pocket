Purpose

This is a private personal budgeting tool operated by Simone Saruggia. It is not a public banking service, payment service or financial adviser. Use this instance only for your own accounts and information you are authorized to access.

Bank connection

Connecting a bank is optional and requires your authorization through Enable Banking and the bank. Their terms and consent conditions apply separately. This app only implements account-information access and cannot move money.

Imports and estimates

Bank data may be delayed, incomplete or corrected by the bank. Pending transactions are not counted. Categorisation and duplicate checks can be wrong. Review uncertain imports, refunds, transfers and currency conversions. Automatic saving is optional and can be turned off. Your bank statement is the authoritative record.

Availability and costs

This personal tool has no uptime guarantee. Bank consent may expire and require reconnecting. Hosting, AI usage and any provider charges remain the operator’s responsibility. No paid service is offered to the public through this instance.

Control

You can disconnect bank access and edit or delete recorded expenses. Protect your private access link. For support, data export or deletion requests, contact simosaruggia@gmail.com. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
